CLASS PULSE - SUA MUC "PHONG CUA TOI" KHONG XUAT HIEN

Nguyen nhan: Ban va truoc thieu index.html (khung giao dien muc Phong cua toi).

1. Trong thu muc website CU (classroom-quiz), sao luu thu muc data (neu co) ra Desktop.
2. Mo thu muc public cua website CU (co index.html, home.js, teacher.js, theme.css).
3. O thu muc ban va moi nay, CHON DUNG 4 file:
   - index.html
   - home.js
   - teacher.js
   - theme.css
   Bam Ctrl+C.
4. Quay ve thu muc classroom-quiz\public CU, bam Ctrl+V.
5. Neu Windows hoi ghi de, chon Replace the files in the destination.
   KHONG copy vao folder public\public. KHONG xoa folder data.
6. Tro ve Chrome tai http://localhost:3000 va bam Ctrl+Shift+R (hoac Ctrl+F5).
7. Keo xuong cuoi trang: se thay "Phong cua toi".
8. Neu bao 0 phong: mo lai tab giao vien cua phong cu, bam Ctrl+F5, roi quay ve trang chu va refresh.

Chi doi 4 file giao dien, khong thay file server.js hay data.
Trang giao dien va giao vien cung cap thong tin phong phu thuoc vao tinh trang dang nhap cua giao vien trong trinh duyet hien tai.
